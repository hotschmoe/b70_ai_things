Single-queue uploads complete before products. Two-queue uploads do NOT prewait:
copy queue copies stable pinned-host weights into independent eight-slot stage;
compute waits for that copied-event, copies it into independent decoded slot,

Compute in-order prevents decoded-slot overwrite ahead of its prior products.

prefill router/stager. Both queues drain before owned memory frees, also on error.
