INDEPENDENT REVIEW: S122/S126, S123, S125

EVIDENCE (compiled or source facts)

- S122 is an actual lowering fact, not a hypothesis. In the selected WideQ5K C2/RPW1 head image, all 16 DP4As are weighted dots. Each of the 4 raw-q8 sums becomes 40 vector ALU instructions (byte packing, sign extension, ADD/ADD3), 160 in the static loop body. Row-owning sub-groups make cross-row reuse impossible.
- This was the reasonable outcome to expect: a compile-time `ones` constant lets the backend fold the emulated `dpct::dp4a` into plain byte adds before the DP4A pattern matcher runs.
- The same helper (`WideQ4K::apply`/`dot`) also serves Q4_K and Q5_K wide paths. The expansion probably exists there too. That is a hypothesis until each image is read.
- S123: MtpPair launches pre_round A, pre_round B and the shared tail serially on one queue. The slot drafters share weights and live on the primary card. The 5 ownership touchpoints (separate weight load, NativeEmbed device alias, per-device queues and graphs, bridges, solo fallback) are real costs. Per-owner forward service is not isolated, and peer idle time is unproven.
- S125: the algebra is feasible, but it needs a second kernel, scratch, 37.5% useful MAC cells by geometry, and an extra 465 MiB for the 29-matrix subset. The same Q6 shapes already got A4 (+0.43% matched serving).

HYPOTHESES (unmeasured)

- Removing the 160-instruction expansion helps only if the head loop is ALU- or issue-limited rather than load-latency-limited. Static counts do not decide this.
- MTP per-depth forwards at C1 may be dominated by weight streaming, in which case reading weights once for both slots matters more than overlapping them.

PRIORITIES AND DECISIONS

1. S126 runtime-uniform ones scalar: proceed with private source preparation, as root selected. It is the cheapest, most direct test of a confirmed compiled inefficiency.
   - Add one cheap alternative spelling to keep in reserve if the scalar is const-folded: load the ones word from a tiny device constant buffer. That is also opaque to compile-time folding.
   - Gate, in order:
     a. The candidate image shows a DP4A whose operand traces to the kernel payload scalar.
     b. The four 40-instruction chains are gone.
     c. The weighted DP4A operands and the FP ledger are unchanged, including the MAD contraction positions on the same operands (lines 388\u2013400 in S122 terms) and the XOR tree.
     d. Resources do not regress.
   - Then the full strict oracle.
   - Stop if: the scalar is folded back into the expansion; or a DP4A appears but the MAD/contraction pattern changes; or new spill or scratch shows up.
   - If it passes, apply the same compiled check to the Q4_K C6 QKV image before anything is generalized. The shared helper does not justify blanket substitution.

2. Structural work: SOURCE_DESIGN_GO for a different, lower-risk variant before S123.
   - **The variant: a single-device paired MTP forward.** Both slots already share MTP weights on one device, and the verifier already runs slot-batched C2 projections. The variant batches A's and B's per-depth forward projections into C2 calls, with attention, KV, positions and residual state kept per owner, so the weights are streamed once per depth instead of twice.
   - **Why it is preferred over S123:** it avoids the cross-context touchpoints (embedding alias, IPC bridges, peer memory, solo-head replication), and it removes the A-then-B serialization in a way that also reduces weight traffic. That last part is a hypothesis.
   - **Design deliverables:**
     - an exact list of projections in `record_front`/`record_rest`/`record_forward` that are slot-independent apart from their activations;
     - which kernels support C2 with distinct column owners;
     - how per-owner KV and attention stay separate;
     - capture keys for unequal T0/T1;
     - preserved fallback when only one slot is active.
   - Stop if: the forward contains per-slot weight-dependent paths (routed MTP experts with per-token routing) that cannot be batched without reordering FP sums; or unequal T or row layouts force a cache-key explosion; or a strict FP-order-preserving C2 MMVQ for these shapes does not already exist.
   - Note: routed experts per token are fine if the grouped kernels already take multiple tokens, which they do for the verifier. This needs confirming from source.

3. S123 cross-device split: SOURCE_DESIGN_HOLD. Revisit only if the paired forward proves impossible, or if an isolated per-owner forward measurement shows the A/B serial span is material and cannot be batched. The NativeEmbed peer-access and solo-fallback head decisions are unresolved blockers, not details.

4. S125 Q6 pre-expansion with DPAS: HOLD.
   - The memory cost is large and the VRAM headroom is historical, not current.
   - The two-kernel structure with scratch is close to the S75 failure mode.
   - These shapes have already yielded a small gain.
   - Revisit only after the ones fix and the paired-forward design are resolved, and only with a measured current free-VRAM budget.

DEV-LOOP NOTES

- Keep S126's first gate compile-and-ISA only. No GPU until the lowering is shown.
- Fold any surviving private candidates (S116, S126) into the next build that is otherwise required, with the persistent offline cache. Each keeps its own single-flag arm.
- Do not attach new private timing runs to candidates whose serving ceiling is likely below 1%. The goal needs roughly 2.7\xd7 per stream, so structural MTP work deserves design time now.
