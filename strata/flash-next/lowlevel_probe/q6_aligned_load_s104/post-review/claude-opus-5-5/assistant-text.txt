# S104 post-result decision

**Verdict: PREPARE_MAIN_INTEGRATION_AFTER_S106.** This is low priority and conditional on S106 closing cleanly. Nothing is integrated or served now.

## Evidence, kept separate

**Measured, finite correctness.**
- All 114 strict full-byte snapshots passed for public, S44, private original and candidate, across both real shapes.
- Coverage: initial and changed Q8, repeats, own graphs (single and 20-call), A/B/A restore, read-only, guards, tails.
- Health and teardown were clean.
- This is bounded to these inputs, shapes and mapping.

**Nested timings.**
- Every paired round improved: out \u22124.5 to \u22125.2%, qkv \u22122.1 to \u22122.4%.
- That is 4 nested paired means per shape, warm, in one process. They are consistent, but they are not independent replications and not serving evidence.
- S100 is the calibration point: about \u221240% in the primitive became about +0.3% in serving.

**Static mechanism.**
- The select chain is gone (SEL 38\u21928).
- DP4A, the FP ledger, the reduction and stores, and the resources are the same as the original.
- This explains a modest primitive change. It predicts nothing about the model.

**Production limits.**
- Main weight pointers are not proven 4-byte aligned, so a host-side fallback is required.
- No main binding, no serving run, and no attribution of these shapes to the critical path.

## Why prepare rather than keep private

- **Small and exact change.** Bit-exact on real weights, guarded by shape, default OFF.
- **Small integration.** One load helper plus one guarded launch branch, beside the existing C6 A2 dispatch.
- **Low risk.** Running is cheap to judge.

Do not expect an end-to-end signal much above noise.

## Blockers for that later proposal (source-only)

1. **Fallback to the existing `a2` launch** whenever `weights % 4 != 0`, or for any shape other than 6144\u21922560 or 2560\u219210240, any column count other than 6, or RPW, PF or center settings outside the tested ones. The guard should be a fallback, not a throw.
2. **Caller proof.** Show that production reaches `launch_q6k_wide<6>` with RPW 1, A2 and PF 0 under the current argv, and that the native weight base pointers are 4-byte aligned, checked by reading the loader or registry source.
3. **Compiled identity after the main build.** The new kernel's ISA must match the reviewed S104 body (loads, FP ledger, resources), and the OFF kernel's prefix must be unchanged.

## Best next development-loop step

1. Finish S106 first, without adding any S104 flag to it.
2. Then prepare the narrow default-OFF patch together with the caller and alignment proof, with no build yet.
3. **Do not run a dedicated serving campaign for A4 alone.** The expected effect is likely below what three pairs can resolve.
4. Instead, give it a single-flag arm in the next scheduled same-binary matched session, with strict output and native-work gates. Report it descriptively only.
5. If root judges that slot not worth its cost, KEEP_PRIVATE is an acceptable fallback, and no further primitive repeats are needed.
