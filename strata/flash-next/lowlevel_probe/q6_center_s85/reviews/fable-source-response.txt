**S85 review: GO_BOUNDED_BUILD.** No blockers found in the packet as written.

**Arithmetic identity (candidate vs original).** Each packed byte after masking is `(vl & 0x0f) | ((vh<<4) & 0x30)`, so u in 0..63. Original: per-byte `sub_sat(u, 0x20)` with u\u221232 in \u221232..31, never saturates, yields signed u\u221232. Candidate: u+96 in 96..159, no byte carry, then XOR 0x80. For u<32 the byte is <128 and XOR adds 128, giving u+224 \u2261 u\u221232 mod 256. For u\u226532 the byte is \u2265128 and XOR subtracts 128, giving u\u221232. Both branches equal the two's-complement of u\u221232, so the dp4a operands are bit-identical. The second expression uses `(vl>>4)&0x0f` and `((vh>>4)<<4)&0x30` identically in both arms, so the sign-extension of `vh` from the arithmetic shift is masked identically and is not a divergence source. The C++20 shift semantics make this defined on both arms.

**Unchanged surfaces.** `load16_a2`, Q8_1 four-int loads, dp4a chain, `acc += ds0*dsc0*s0 + ds1*dsc1*s1` FP ledger, XOR permute tree, SG32 / NCOLS=6 / RPW=1, and the launch geometry (`groups=ceil(M/4)`, four sub-groups per group, uniform `row0>=n_out` early return) are shared verbatim between `CAND=false` and `CAND=true`. Block layout static_asserts are correct (210 bytes, `d` at 208, Q8_1 qs at 4). Only the two `if constexpr(CAND)` expressions differ.
