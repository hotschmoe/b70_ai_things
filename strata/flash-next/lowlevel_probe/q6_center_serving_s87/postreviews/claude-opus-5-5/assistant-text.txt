**ACCEPT_DECISION** (RETAIN_DEFAULT_OFF_OPTIN, bounded), with the overclaim cautions below.

**Mechanism (source/ISA/primitive), separate from serving**
- The AOT audit is exact at the primitive level. Both kernels use SIMD32, GRF128 and 48 DP4A. In the candidate, 32 saturating sequences become 8 packed adds plus 8 XOR, and kernel text shrinks from 16576 to 10880 B.
- Spill fields are *absent*, not zero. That is not proof of no spill.
- None of this shows the candidate kernel was dispatched on the GPU per window. The activation scope correctly limits itself to host marker, AOT binding, and no errors.

**Observable equivalence before timing: satisfied**
- All four pairs (warm plus repeat 1\u20133) pass every check: request, full text, SSE content, usage, native cache/prompt/predicted (0/107/1500), draft and accepted, windows/paired/solo, reported rows, and logged new captures.
- Draft work differs between repeats (1120/1128/1122/1110\u20131138) but is identical within each pair. That is why within-pair comparison is valid and cross-repeat pooling is weaker.
- Logged captures are equal in text only, and only in the warm pair and repeat3. They do not prove per-window layout identity or capture-cache hits.
- SSE event counts (1330\u20131335 for 1500 tokens) confirm that events carry multiple tokens. Gap statistics are therefore per event, not per token, and are not usable as token latency.

**Timing evidence**
- All three timed pairs agree in direction: wall time \u22121.14, \u22121.24 and \u22121.27%; aggregate throughput +1.15, +1.25 and +1.28%.
- Per-stream TTFT (about \u22120.04 s) and decode (+0.6 tok/s) move the same way. The consistency is real but small.
- Weaknesses:
  - The single process order (OFF then ON) confounds the result with thermal state, page cache and drift.
  - The warm pair is included in the all-four list but correctly excluded from the median.
  - n=3 means the p95 values are just interpolations of three points.
  - TRACE1 CPU logging was on in both arms. It is matched, not zero-overhead, and not a device profile.
- An effect of about 1.2% is within what an order effect could plausibly produce. "Bounded matched gain" is the strongest defensible label; "speedup" is not.
- The S85 projection of about 22% must not be cited. The roughly 20\xd7 gap between projection and observation itself suggests the verify kernel is a minor share of wall time.

**Overclaim check**
- None found in the packet. The aggregate-proof `requests` miscount and the common-prefetch counter caveats are self-corrected.
- Nothing supports full-context qualification, daily-shelf promotion or default-on, and none is asserted.
- The goal status (about 54\u201356 tok/s per stream, versus a 150 target) is reported honestly.

**Retain opt-in vs park**
- Retaining as default-off opt-in is justified: exact equivalence, consistent sign, a smaller kernel, and no health regressions.
- Parking would discard a clean, cheap primitive win.
- Promotion is not justified until order is controlled.

**Most useful next discriminator**
- If anyone wants to firm up this candidate, the cheapest step is one reversed-order process pair (ON then OFF), reusing the existing binary and configs. That costs about 2\xd738 s of startup plus about 4\xd729 s of pairs, with no build.
  - If the sign holds, the order confound is retired.
  - If it flips, park the candidate.

- No cache campaign or repeat inflation is warranted.
