**HC mechanism review**

The candidate moves one scalar expression from the UP prologue, where every one of 160 groups evaluates it for every active row, into the reduce epilogue, where each row is already owned by exactly one thread. UP then loads the published value into its local array and continues unchanged. No launch is removed, no global synchronisation is added, and the four kernel boundaries stay.

Arithmetic ledger, checked against the excerpts:

- Reduce produces x by the existing 80-slice sum. UP today computes y equal to 0 plus the stored x, divides by 4, and applies x over 1 plus exp of minus x. The leading zero-plus is an identity on x except for negative zero, and x cannot be negative zero because the sum starts from positive zero and an exact cancellation rounds to positive zero. The store and load of x through the part buffer is exact. So the candidate epilogue, written with the identical statement sequence including the zero-plus, division by the float HC constant, unary minus, native exp, add one and division, is bit-equal in source terms.
- The remaining risk is lowering, not algebra. Native exp may be mapped differently in two kernels, and the compiler may contract or reorder differently. That is a compiled gate, not a source argument.
- Nonfinite and extreme inputs behave identically in both places if the ops are the same: large negative x yields negative zero, NaN propagates, subnormal x survives the division under the same control register. Signed zero of v is the one place where producer placement could matter and it must be in the fixture.

Ownership and alias:

- lo is a caller span of 320 floats per token, disjoint per token in both the drafter and verifier arenas. Today UP group 0 writes it after UP's own computation. The candidate has reduce write it before UP reads it, on the same in-order queue with the existing reduce-to-UP edge. No reader sits between them in the sliced multi path.
- part keeps its raw write. UP stops reading it but the fixture must still compare it.
- The candidate must be selected only when nsplit is 1, which is the sliced path. The direct multi-split path, MORE, GRF256, ILP and the single-kernel pair keep their original producers.
- The fixture must allocate lo, part, part2, inputs and outputs as disjoint guarded spans. Production alias safety is a separate callsite proof.

**Verdict: HC_SOURCE_PREP_GO for one private strict oracle preparation, after the ISA read below.**
