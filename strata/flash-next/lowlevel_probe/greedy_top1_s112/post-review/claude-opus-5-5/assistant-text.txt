# S112 post-result decision

## Disposition

- **Accept** the bounded result.
- **Correctness:** 48 strict cases passed, with exact IDs and identical downstream bytes, across cross-work-group ties and nonfinite values, with counters persisting across observations.
- **Timing:** the private two-owner chain went from about 165 \xb5s to 116 \xb5s (about \u221249 \xb5s), in nested ABBA rounds within one process.
- **What it is not:** model speed or target progress. For calibration, the post-head chain runs about twice per C2 cycle against a cycle of roughly 57 ms. Expect a small serving effect, in the same class as Q6 A4 (+0.43%). Do not compute a percentage from the old S108 timings.

## Priority for the next ~6 hours

- **GPU window: B first.** It needs no build, uses the existing audited binary, and targets prefill, which has a larger unmet goal. Its stop-on-first-mismatch gate is cheap.
- **In parallel on CPU:** prepare A's source and do its CPU build, then the compiled audit. Run A's serving comparison in the next GPU window after B.
- **C:** defer. Its ceiling is lower, and it must not be combined with A.
- **D:** limit to source mapping of the head (about 1.2 ms per depth) and HC paths, looking for a structural mechanism, not knobs.

## B gates

- **Configuration:** the same binary for both arms, with suffix 0, lookup 0, spec 4, MTP max T 4 and min-p 0 fixed in both. All other flags OFF.
- **Stop rule:** stop on the first difference in text, SSE, usage, native work or captures. A mismatch is recorded as unresolved, not as a new attribution.
- **Run:** one unmeasured 4K warm-up, then the original 3 \xd7 4K and 3 \xd7 32K inputs, then functionals, QUIT and health.
- **Claims:** a pass shows the activation and matched work under the fixed policy only. Prefill timings are descriptive. It does not fix the S94 cause and gives no default change.

## A: smallest integration design

1. **Header.** Add the S112 private header **byte-identical** to the one tested, in its unique namespace, and include it only from `mtp.cpp`.
   - This duplicates the algorithm, but it keeps the kernel body exactly as tested and leaves `verify_kernels`, the CUDA-style selector and the default memory order untouched.
2. **Selection** in `MtpDrafter::record_post_head`. The branch requires:
   - a process-latched, default-OFF environment flag, read before any capture;
   - `!sub` (full head only);
   - T = 1, unpenalized greedy, and `argmax_rows_wanted()` false.

   Otherwise use the original `sample_tokens`. The probability, mapping and select calls stay unchanged.
3. **Scratch.** Reuse each owner's `arg_scratch_`, which is zero-filled at init and otherwise unused on B70. Assert its size against `sizeof(s112_private::ArgRow)`.
4. **Marker:** once, at the first eligible use; host selection only.

**Pre-GPU gates:**
- an incremental build;
- the new main kernel's ISA matching the reviewed S112 candidate in its publication sequence, compares, store and reset, and its resources;
- the OFF sampler/probability/select images unchanged.

**Serving gate:** the same new binary, flag 0/1, with fixed-work flags in both arms, and strict text, SSE, usage, native work and captures. Then functionals, QUIT and health.

## What stops A early

- Any ID, work or capture mismatch.
- A launch rejection, for example from `use_root_sync` cooperative limits.
- A main-binary ISA that departs from the reviewed publication order.
- A counter left nonzero after failure. There is no reset-only recovery.
- A serving delta within the range of the earlier small results: keep default OFF and do not repeat. The 150 tok/s per-stream and prefill targets remain unmet regardless.
